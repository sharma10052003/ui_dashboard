Introduction

Healthcare management is becoming increasingly important in modern life. As schedules become tighter, individuals often neglect vital routines.

Many people forget medications, adequate hydration, or daily fitness activities. The Smart Health Reminder App is designed to seamlessly solve these problems.

By leveraging automated reminders, smart notifications, and comprehensive health tracking, users can effortlessly maintain their wellbeing.

Problem Statement

  • Missed Medications: People frequently forget to take medicines on time, leading to complications.
  • No Monitoring: A general lack of daily health and wellness monitoring.
  • Poor Adherence: Difficulty adhering strictly to prescribed medical routines.
  • Fitness Neglect: Difficulty consistently tracking and maintaining fitness activities.

OTP Verification

Zero-Trust Security Model

To guarantee maximum security for sensitive health data, users must verify their identity using a One-Time Password (OTP).

This dynamic code is securely transmitted to the user's registered email or phone, offering robust account protection against unauthorized access.
